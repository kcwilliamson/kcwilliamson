# Hi, I'm Katie Williamson.

I design product experiences, content systems, and AI-ready information architectures that make complex things clearer, more useful, and easier to operate at scale.

This profile is where I share experiments, prototypes, and working notes from the intersection of UX strategy, content design, systems thinking, and useful AI.

I'm less interested in novelty for its own sake than in the theory-practice loop: how ideas become operating models, how structure changes behavior, and how better information design helps both people and machines make better decisions.

A lot of my side projects are lightweight prototypes. I use them to pressure-test interaction patterns, explore conversational design, and turn practical experimentation into reusable principles.
